ScrollLock Privacy Policy

Mindful Instagram Screen Time & Scroll Tracker

✓ Zero Cloud Storage ✓ 100% Local Device Processing ✓ Strict Accessibility Scoping Effective Date: November 25, 2026

🛡️ Introduction & Summary

At ScrollLock, we believe that taking control of your screen time should never compromise your digital privacy. This Privacy Policy details our core commitment: ScrollLock is designed as an offline-first, local-only utility.

Core Privacy Promise: ScrollLock does not operate any remote database, user account server, or cloud storage. None of your personal data, scroll history, or activity logs are ever transmitted to or stored on any external server or cloud backend.

💾 Data Collection & Local-Only Storage

All operational data utilized by ScrollLock is saved solely on your physical device using Android's native private storage (SharedPreferences):

  • Daily Scroll Count: An integer tally representing the number of scrolls performed in Instagram today.
  • Daily Scroll Limit: Your chosen limit threshold (e.g. 5, 10, or custom).
  • Last Reset Date: A local date marker (YYYY-MM-DD) used by the midnight automatic counter reset logic.
  • User Preference Settings: Toggles such as whether to reset your limit to default daily.

This information stays exclusively within your device's sandbox. It is never uploaded, synced, backed up to third-party clouds, or shared with any entity.

♿ Android Accessibility Service Disclosure

To provide its core functionality, ScrollLock requires you to explicitly enable its Accessibility Service (ScrollLockAccessibilityService). In accordance with Google Play's Developer Program Policies regarding the Accessibility API, we provide the following explicit disclosures:

  • Purpose of Use: The Accessibility Service is used solely to detect scroll events (TYPE_VIEW_SCROLLED) within Instagram feeds and monitor window state transitions (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED) to display the floating scroll counter and mindful lock reminder overlay.
  • Strict Scoping to Instagram: ScrollLock explicitly restricts its accessibility filters in configuration and runtime code to Instagram packages (com.instagram.android and com.instagram.lite). It does not receive events from or monitor any other application on your phone.
  • No Sensitive Data Access: ScrollLock does NOT record, collect, read, or transmit your passwords, text inputs, keystrokes, personal messages, banking or UPI details, contact numbers, or photos.
  • No Audio/Call Interception: The service does not alter phone settings, record audio, or intercept phone calls.
  • No Screen Recording: ScrollLock does not take screenshots or capture video recordings of your display.
Scroll events detected by the service are immediately incremented into a numeric local counter and discarded from memory. No context of what content was scrolled is ever evaluated or saved.

🪟 Display Over Other Apps (Overlay Permission)

ScrollLock requests the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission to draw floating UI elements:

  • Floating Counter Pill: Displays your live scroll count (e.g., "3 / 5 scrolls") while browsing Instagram feeds so you stay mindfully aware.
  • Full-Screen Lock Overlay: Displays a break reminder when your chosen daily limit is reached.

Strict Overlay Lifecycle: Overlays are rendered strictly when Instagram is active. Whenever you leave Instagram, exit to the Home screen, or open other applications (such as Google Pay, banking apps, or messaging), all overlay views are immediately dismissed from Android's WindowManager.

📢 Third-Party Services & Google AdMob SDK

ScrollLock may integrate third-party developer software development kits (SDKs), specifically the Google AdMob SDK (provided by Google LLC), to serve advertisements and perform basic app health analytics:

  • Ad Serving & Analytics: Google AdMob may automatically collect and process non-personally identifiable diagnostic and usage data, such as:
    • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
    • Device information (device model, operating system version, carrier, language).
    • General, non-precise coarse location (country or city level derived from IP address).
    • Ad interaction events and crash diagnostic telemetry.
  • Privacy Compliance: Google processes this information under its own privacy policies. You can learn more about how Google handles ad data by visiting:
    Google Privacy & Terms and AdMob Privacy & Data Collection Guidelines.
  • Opt-Out: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by visiting Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID.

💳 Financial & Banking Apps (Google Pay / UPI) Compatibility

Payment and banking apps (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, and banking portals) incorporate automated security scans that check for active accessibility services and screen overlays to defend against remote-access malware:

Security Assurance: ScrollLock cannot observe, read, or interact with Google Pay, UPI transactions, or banking PINs. Furthermore, ScrollLock immediately clears all overlays whenever payment apps are in focus.

If your payment app flags active accessibility services during local development (common for sideloaded APKs), you can easily pause or toggle ScrollLock's Accessibility Service off in your phone's Accessibility Settings before making transactions.

🗑️ Data Retention & Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can reset your daily scroll count to 0 at any time via the in-app "Reset Today's Count to 0" button.
  • You can erase all app data and custom limit preferences by going to Android Settings > Apps > ScrollLock > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling ScrollLock immediately and permanently deletes all data associated with the application from your device.

👶 Children's Privacy

If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate steps.
